Put everything in one place, for the people you trust.

LegacyFolio helps you identify and organize the important information, photos, and documents your substitute decision-makers and executors will need to care for you and manage your estate. It’s organized to remind you of the details that are easy to forget.

Accounts. Insurance. Property. Medical details. Legal documents. Passwords. Final wishes.

When you’re ready, LegacyFolio creates your Folio: a well-organized, password-protected PDF that you can share with the people you trust when and how you choose.

LegacyFolio can gently remind you to keep your Folio current, and flag any records that are out of date.

The people you trust don’t need the LegacyFolio app to read your Folio. They can open it in any regular web browser or PDF reader with the password you provide, and find what they need through a clickable table of contents.

You’re in control.

You choose what goes in your Folio, including photos and PDF documents.

You can update and share your Folio as often as you like, with the same password or a different one.

You choose when and how to share your Folio and its password, together or separately.

For example, you could send quarterly updates of your Folio to your executor or substitute decision-maker, and leave the password with your lawyer to share when the time comes.

No strings attached.

Your Data Belongs to You.

LegacyFolio collects no data and requires no account. We never access or store your information, photos, or documents. Your data stays on your devices and, if you choose to sync, in your private Apple iCloud.

Yours to Keep.

Your Folio is a standard PDF file. It opens in any web browser or PDF reader, today and years from now, without using the LegacyFolio app.
