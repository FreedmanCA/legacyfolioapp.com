About LegacyFolio

What is LegacyFolio?

LegacyFolio is a digital legacy binder and end-of-life planner that helps you collect the information, photos, and documents your executors and substitute decision-makers will need to manage your affairs and estate. It brings them together in a Folio: a well-organized, password-protected PDF file that you share with the people you trust.

Why is LegacyFolio different from a document, notes, or a spreadsheet?

A document or spreadsheet is blank. You have to decide what to include, and you won’t know what you’ve missed. LegacyFolio gives you categories with a guide to what each one can hold. It flags missing basic details and reminds you to review records that may be out of date.

You can add photos and documents to each record. LegacyFolio contains a Directory of people, pets, places, and organizations, so you enter each one once and link it to every record that needs it. Change a detail in one place and it’s right everywhere.

When you’re ready, LegacyFolio turns everything into a digital Folio, a password-protected PDF that the people you trust can open without the app. You can give your Folio to as many recipients as you like.

When things change, update your records and create a new Folio with the press of a button. Share it using your current Folio Password or a new one. Older Folios keep the Folio Password they were created with.

Do the people I share my Folio with need the app?

No. Your Folio recipients open your Folio in any regular web browser or PDF reader, using the password you give them.

Does LegacyFolio collect my personal information?

No. LegacyFolio collects no data and requires no account.

Which devices does LegacyFolio work on?

LegacyFolio works on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. One universal license covers all three. If you use iCloud sync, your information stays current across all your Apple devices signed in to the same iCloud account.

Privacy and Security

Where is my information stored?

Your information stays on your devices and, if you choose to sync, in your private Apple iCloud. LegacyFolio does not store any data on its own servers, and it never shares anything automatically.

Can anyone at LegacyFolio see my information?

No. LegacyFolio collects no data and requires no account, so we have no access to your records, photos, documents, or Folios. We also have no copy of your passwords, and we cannot recover them.

Is my information encrypted?

ID and account numbers, access and recovery codes, seed phrases, medical details, legacy letters, and your Message to Reader are end-to-end encrypted in iCloud. For everything else, Apple holds the keys unless you turn on Apple’s Advanced Data Protection (ADP).

On a Mac, turn on FileVault to encrypt the data stored on the Mac itself. You can find it in System Settings > Privacy & Security > FileVault. Many newer Macs turn it on automatically.

Should I turn on Advanced Data Protection?

ADP applies end-to-end encryption to all of your iCloud data, so that Apple cannot access it. It also means only you can recover your data. If you lose access to your devices and your recovery method, your data cannot be restored, not even by Apple. If you turn on ADP, set up a recovery key or recovery contact.

What protects LegacyFolio if someone else uses my device?

Your App Password locks LegacyFolio, and you can also use Face ID or Touch ID. In Settings, you choose how soon LegacyFolio locks after you leave it.

Is my Folio protected when I share it?

Yes. Every Folio is a password-protected PDF, so anyone who receives the file also needs the Folio Password to open it. For extra protection, give the Folio and its password by different methods.

Does address search send information anywhere?

When you search for an address, what you type is sent to Apple Maps to find matches. Address search is optional. You can turn off address search in Settings and type addresses yourself.

Does LegacyFolio change my Contacts?

No. LegacyFolio only reads Contacts when you import a person’s details. It never changes them. If the details change in Contacts, LegacyFolio can alert you so you can choose which changes to accept. You can turn off these alerts in Settings.

Passwords

What passwords does LegacyFolio use?

  • App Password: Locks the app. You can also use Face ID or Touch ID.
  • Folio Password: Protects your Folio. You give it to the people who will read your Folio. To see your current Folio Password, print a Folio Password Card.
  • Archive Password: Protects your Archives, the encrypted copies of your data.

All three passwords are stored in your iCloud Keychain, so they are available on every device signed in to your iCloud account.

What if I forget a password?

Password Warning: LegacyFolio cannot recover your passwords, and reinstalling the app will not reset your App Password. Choose passwords you will remember.

If I change my Folio Password, will my older Folios still open?

Yes, but with the old password. A new Folio Password applies only to Folios created after the change. Each Folio opens with the password it was created with.

Your Folio

What is in my Folio?

  • A cover and a table of contents with clickable links.
  • Your Message to Reader, if you have written one.
  • Your records, organized by category, with numbered pages.
  • Your attachments, all included at the end of the Folio.

Can I see how a record will look in my Folio?

Yes. Every record has a Preview Folio button that shows how the record will appear in your Folio. You can hide the Preview Folio button in Settings.

Can I review my Folio before I share it?

Yes. Creating a Folio does not share it. LegacyFolio never shares anything automatically. Create your Folio, open it, and review it as often as you like. When you are happy with it, share it with the people you trust. If you find something to change, update your records and create a new Folio.

How do I share my Folio?

You decide when and how. You can give someone your Folio and its password at the same time, or at different times, or by different methods. For example, you could send quarterly updates of your Folio to your executors or substitute decision-makers, and leave the Folio Password with your lawyer to share when the time comes.

Your Folio is a standard PDF file, so you can share it the same way you share any other file. LegacyFolio shows the approximate size of your Folio before you create it, which can help you choose a method:

  • Email: Attach your Folio to an email. Most email services limit attachments to about 20–25 MB.
  • Mail Drop: For larger Folios, Apple Mail can send the file using Mail Drop, which uploads it to iCloud and emails a download link. Files up to 5 GB can be sent, and the link expires after 30 days, so ask your recipient to download and save the Folio.
  • AirDrop or Messages: Send your Folio directly to someone nearby with AirDrop, or in a message.
  • Cloud Storage: Upload your Folio to a service such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, or OneDrive, and share a link. Anyone with the link can download the Folio, but they still need the Folio Password to open it.
  • USB Drive: Save your Folio to a USB drive and give it to someone in person, or store it with your important documents.
  • Printed Copy: Print your Folio and give someone the paper copy. A printed Folio is not password-protected, so store it carefully. See Can I print my Folio? below.

For extra protection, give the Folio and its password by different methods. For example, email the Folio and give the password in person.

LegacyFolio can also print a Folio Password Card that shows the password and tells the reader how to open the Folio.

Can I print my Folio?

Yes. Open your Folio in any web browser or PDF reader, enter the Folio Password, and print it like any other document. When you create your Folio, you can choose a larger text size if it will be read on paper.

You can also give someone a printed Folio instead of an electronic copy, for example someone who prefers to read on paper.

Printing Warning: A printed Folio is not password-protected. Anyone who has the paper copy can read it, so store it as carefully as your other important documents.

How do I keep my Folio up to date?

Update your records whenever something changes, such as a new account, a new address, or a change to your will. Then create a new Folio with the press of a button and give it to the people you trust. You can use your current Folio Password or choose a new one.

LegacyFolio helps you stay current. It flags records that are missing basic details or haven’t been updated in a while, and it can remind you to review your information on a schedule you choose.

Will my Folio still open if I stop using LegacyFolio?

Yes. Your Folio is a standard, password-protected PDF file. It opens in any web browser or PDF reader, today and years from now, without the LegacyFolio app.

Do I still need to keep my original documents?

Yes. You can attach photos and PDFs of your documents, but your substitute decision-makers and executors may need the originals. Keep your original documents in safekeeping, and record where they are stored in LegacyFolio.

Sync, Storage, and Archives

Can I use LegacyFolio without iCloud?

Yes. iCloud sync is optional. If you turn it off, all data stays on the device where you entered it and doesn’t appear on your other devices.

If your data is not synced using iCloud, an Archive is your backup. Export one regularly in Settings and keep it somewhere safe, so you can restore your data if you lose your device or delete the app.

What is an Archive?

An Archive is an encrypted copy of all your LegacyFolio data. An Archive is protected by an Archive Password, and only LegacyFolio can open it. Archive controls are in Settings.

Archives are especially useful if you don’t use iCloud sync. Export an Archive to back up your data. Import an Archive to restore your data to LegacyFolio. Importing only adds missing data to LegacyFolio. It never deletes or overwrites what you already have.

Does LegacyFolio use my iCloud storage?

Yes, if you use iCloud sync. Your data is stored in your own private iCloud, so it counts against your Apple iCloud storage. LegacyFolio optimizes photos to save space.

What happens if I get a new iPhone, iPad, or Mac?

  • With iCloud Sync: Your data is stored in your private Apple iCloud, so it is available on your new device when you sign in to the same iCloud account.
  • Without iCloud Sync: Export an Archive on your old device and import it on your new one.

What happens if I delete the app or lose my device?

Data Warning: If iCloud sync is off, your data exists only on the device where you entered it. If you delete the app or lose the device, your data cannot be recovered unless you have a previously exported Archive. If iCloud sync is on, your data remains in your iCloud.

Can I recover a record I deleted?

The 30-Day Rule: A deleted record goes to Recently Deleted for 30 days, where you can restore it. After 30 days it is permanently deleted. Deleting an attachment is immediate and permanent.

Pricing & Limits

What are the limits of the free and paid licenses?

  • Free License: A free license allows creation of a Folio with up to 10 records.
  • Paid License: A paid license removes the record limit. There is no annual fee.
  • iCloud Storage & Sync: iCloud storage and sync capacity is determined by your personal Apple iCloud subscription and available space.

One universal license covers your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
